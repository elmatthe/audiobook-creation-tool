## Why some previews are blocked

- **Edge voices** come from Microsoft's online service; its terms govern redistributing the audio and have not been confirmed.
- **Chatterbox and Qwen3 cloned voices** are synthetic voices made from bundled reference recordings of other people. Redistribution rights to those recordings, and so to audio cloned from them, have not been audited.
- **Kokoro and Qwen3 preset voices** are generated by local models whose license is Apache-2.0 (evidence recorded per voice in `previews.json`).

## How they are made

Each preview is produced by the app's own engine for that voice (not a separate path), with leading and trailing silence trimmed to 120 ms, saved as 24 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, and checked for length (8-13 s), clipping and silence. `previews.json` records every voice's rights status and basis, and each preview's SHA-256 and duration. Regenerate or validate with `python files/dev-tools/voice_previews.py generate | check | status`.

These files live in the repository only. They are not part of the release archives, and no preview is rendered for the voices the maintainer excluded from the app.
