> **22 of 28 previews are available.** The other 6 are **pending generation**: publication is authorized, but the audio cannot be made yet (see "Pending previews"). This folder is not a complete set until every row is available.

The sentence: *Welcome. This is a short sample of my voice, reading a calm passage at an even pace, so that you can decide whether it suits the story you want to hear, today or tomorrow.*

## Publication decision

On 2026-10-20 the owner decided: Short generated preview audio for all 28 registered ACT voices may be published in files/voice-previews/. This supersedes the earlier restriction: The 2026-10-19 Q5/Q8 restriction on preview audio for the Edge, Chatterbox and Qwen3 clone voices (kept as history in md-instructions/Decisions.md).

- **Scope:** Generated preview audio only. Not covered: private reference transcripts, the reference recordings, any untracked or private asset, any voice that is not one of the 28 registered voices.
- **What this is:** An owner publication decision. This project did not independently verify any third-party license or service terms for the Edge, Chatterbox or Qwen3 clone voices, and makes no claim about them.
- **Kokoro and the two Qwen3 preset voices** are generated by local models whose license is Apache-2.0 (the evidence is recorded per voice in `previews.json`).
- **Separate gate:** A Qwen3 clone preview is only generated from an owner-approved transcript bound to its reference recording's SHA-256.
- **Separate gate:** Qwen3 production-clone qualification (S5) is unchanged by this decision.

## How they are made

Each preview is produced by the app's own engine for that voice (not a separate path), with leading and trailing silence trimmed to 120 ms, saved as 24 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, and checked for length (8-13 s), clipping and silence. The Edge previews are a snapshot of Microsoft's online service on the date recorded in `previews.json`. That file records every voice's publication basis, and each preview's SHA-256, duration and date. Regenerate or validate with `python files/dev-tools/voice_previews.py generate | check | status`.

These files live in the repository only. They are not part of the release archives, and no preview is rendered for the voices the maintainer excluded from the app.
